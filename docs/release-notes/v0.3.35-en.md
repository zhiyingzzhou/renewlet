# Renewlet v0.3.35

Renewlet v0.3.35 adds account balance tracking, updates manual renewals, and improves AI recognition. Thanks to [@rysyd](https://github.com/rysyd) for requesting balance tracking ([#101](https://github.com/zhiyingzzhou/renewlet/issues/101)) and [@Flying-Ace](https://github.com/Flying-Ace) for the feedback on manual renewals ([#102](https://github.com/zhiyingzzhou/renewlet/issues/102)).

## Account balances

- Enable balance tracking when adding or editing a subscription to enter its balance and currency. Each subscription has its own balance, shown on its card and in its details. The balance currency can differ from the subscription's price currency.
- Balances are updated manually. Renewals do not deduct from them, and they do not count toward spending. Imports, exports, and backups include recorded balances.

## Manual renewals

- Choose **Renew** from a subscription's **⋯ menu** to renew before the due date or after it expires, without reactivating it first.
- Renewals default to **Continue billing cycle**, including expired subscriptions. Early renewals advance from the existing due date; overdue renewals advance to the next cycle date after today. Choose **Restart subscription** if you want a new start date.
- Repeated clicks cannot add an extra renewal. If a network error leaves the result unclear, you will be prompted to refresh and check before trying again.

## Other changes

- AI recognition accepts text and screenshots together, without switching input modes.
- For fixed-term services with a known purchase date and term, AI recognition can fill in the end date and determine whether the service has expired.
- Failed saves keep your input when adding, editing, or renewing a subscription. Background refreshes no longer overwrite your changes.
- Fixed date controls overlapping the buttons at the bottom of the renewal dialog on small screens.

## Upgrading

- Refresh any open pages after upgrading.
- The **OpenAI** provider now uses the Responses API. For third-party services that only support Chat Completions, select **OpenAI Compatible** in AI settings.
