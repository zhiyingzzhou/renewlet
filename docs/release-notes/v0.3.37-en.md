# Renewlet v0.3.37

For a weekly subscription, you can now see the next few weeks on the calendar before renewing this week. Thanks to [@Flying-Ace](https://github.com/Flying-Ace) for the example in [#108](https://github.com/zhiyingzzhou/renewlet/issues/108).

[中文 ->](./v0.3.37-zh.md)

---

## Calendar and dashboard

- The calendar shows each renewal due in the month you're viewing. Later dates are marked **Estimated**. Viewing them doesn't record a payment or change the subscription's saved billing date.
- **Upcoming renewals** on the dashboard lists the next five renewals or expirations in date order. Select **View calendar** to see further ahead.

## Other changes

- **Expected charges this month** now includes every charge still due this month, so a weekly bill is no longer counted just once. For subscriptions due on the 31st, the March estimate falls on the 31st again after February.
- On mobile, the start and due dates on a subscription card now stay on the same line when there's room for both.
